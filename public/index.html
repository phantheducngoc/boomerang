A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Easy to learn.
Hard to put down.

W A S D

Find your feet. Move with WASD or arrow keys.

CLICK

Let it fly. Your boomerang appears 2 seconds after the round starts. Aim with your mouse. Tap for a short throw or hold left click to increase range, then release. Your character faces the throw direction; cover can shorten its flight. More charge means more speed and range. Your boomerang returns unless parried or blocked by cover—hold the throw button to recall your dropped weapon, or walk near it to pick it up automatically.

F / RIGHT CLICK

Up close and personal. Strike in the direction of your aim. Works even while your boomerang is away, with a short cooldown. Cover blocks strikes. Strike an incoming boomerang to knock it away and onto the ground; parried weapons are harmless until retrieved by their owner.

SPACE

Make a little escape. Press Space or controller A or R (RB/R1) to dash. Release and press again after the cooldown; holding does not repeat. You’re invincible during the dash.

♜

Last legend standing. One hit or a fall into the water takes you out. Win five rounds to take the match. Cover turns outgoing throws back. Returning throws stop and drop when they hit cover; walk nearby to pick up yours automatically.

Keyboard/mouse or a standard gamepad: left stick moves and aims, right stick overrides aim, Y (top face button) charges or recalls; release to throw. A (bottom face button) or R (RB/R1, right shoulder) dashes. X/B (left/right face buttons) strike. Keyboard E retrieves. On a phone or iPad, the left stick moves and aims; Throw, Hit, and Dash buttons appear on the arena. Connect a controller via USB or Bluetooth and press a button to detect it. Use the mouse for lobby menus. Controller access requires localhost or HTTPS.
